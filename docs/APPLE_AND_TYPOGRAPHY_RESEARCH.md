# Optimix — Apple web principles and multilingual type research

Research date: 24 September 2026. Scope: visual direction for Phase 04, not a final interface, font commitment, or implementation. This note supplements the broader reference matrix and art direction. Phase 03 defines a content-led homepage, a conditional procurement demonstration page, and a contact page; the visual rules below should make those routes clearer without turning the demonstration into a claimed client result.

## Method and evidence limits

I opened and scrolled the live pages in a browser at approximately 1265 × 710 desktop viewport. I inspected the rendered page and accessibility state, including a MacBook Pro highlight-gallery selection. Static page structure was cross-checked against the live Apple pages. The browser did not expose a viewport override in this session, so **mobile behavior was not observed directly**. Motion timings, frame rates, implementation technology, and performance were **not measured**. Visual interpretations and proposed Optimix applications are identified below as inferences.

| Live page | Direct observation | Meaning for Optimix (inference) | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MacBook Pro](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/) | Dark hero places a large angled product render beside a short, prominent statement, subordinate product detail, and one buying action. During scrolling, a compact local navigation stays at the top. The following “Get the highlights” area changes to a dark charcoal surface and exposes a wide horizontal gallery with labelled tabs and a play/pause control. Selecting “Battery life” set that tab selected and moved the associated image/text panel into view; neighboring panels remain partially visible. A later “Take a closer look” heading resets the story before detail. | Make each major section answer one question, change visual density between narrative beats, and give any workflow sequence direct controls and a stable nonanimated state. Treat deliberate pause between sections as hierarchy. | Do not copy the device silhouette, black product-launch hero, chip imagery, gallery geometry, purchase pill, or product category navigation. Its long page and video production are justified by a product launch, not Optimix’s current content. |
| [iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/) | Hero animation reveals the phone against a black field; typography and offer appear as separate visual layers. Scrolling changes to a “Get the highlights” area with large photographic content. At one sampled point after a scroll, the entering section was almost blank, then its heading and image appeared in a subsequent capture. | Motion can reveal causality or direct attention, but essential Optimix copy must be present without waiting for a transition. A complete static state should work immediately and under reduced motion. | Do not adopt cinematic phone choreography, giant “PRO” letters, exact easing, or a dark aesthetic across the whole site. | [Apple at Work](https://www.apple.com/business/) | Hero uses multiple photographs of people in different work settings, followed by a very large headline and supporting text. ### What was observed about the system

- **Typography:** hierarchy is established by strong differences between short display headlines, subheads, and quieter explanation. The most prominent phrases are short enough to read as one thought. This is a visual observation, not an instruction to copy Apple’s exact font sizes, tracking, line breaks, or SF typefaces.
- **Whitespace and composition:** major ideas occupy distinct visual fields. Large imagery gets room to function as a focal point; denser comparison information appears only after the opening idea. Alignment and repeated content edges keep transitions coherent. The business page is denser than the product pages, so “one visual idea” is best understood as **one dominant decision or subject at a time**, not literally one element per viewport.
- **Imagery:** product pages use art-directed object imagery; the business page uses recognizably real human work settings. The transferable property is evidence-led art direction and careful cropping. For Optimix, photography should depict an actual operational situation or a verified representation of one, not generic office stock.
- **Motion and interaction:** the visited pages use reveal transitions, animated hero media, gallery progression, explicit gallery tabs, and play/pause controls. A selected tab had an observable state change. Motion can organize a sequence, but a visitor should never need animation to discover the underlying text, action, or workflow state.
- **Storytelling:** the MacBook Pro page moves from product proposition to highlights to deeper capability; Apple at Work moves from broad business context to evidence and use cases. For Optimix, the corresponding story must follow its own Phase 03 path: operational friction → controlled process change → concrete demonstration → economic evaluation → enquiry.

### Principles adopted; copying forbidden

| Adopt the discipline | Optimix-specific translation | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| One dominant message at a time | Each section has a single visitor question and one strongest visual proof; supportive detail stays available. | Apple page structure, slogans, line breaks, and exact proportions. |
| Deliberate pacing | Alternate concise statements, a substantive workflow view, and compact explanatory passages according to the information architecture. | A product-launch sequence of large empty scenes or obligatory pinned scrolling. |
| Art-directed, relevant imagery | Show document handoffs, approvals, inventory/operations, or actual systems only when rights and factual context are clear. | Apple product imagery or anonymous “happy people with laptops” stock. |
| Interaction with visible state | Workflow steps can be selected; the current stage, human owner, system action, and exception route remain clear. | Apple’s carousel geometry and animation choreography. |
| Motion explains a change | A transition can show a manual handoff becoming a controlled flow; static before/after remains sufficient. | Autoplay spectacle, essential information hidden until scroll reveal, or exact Apple timing. |
| Evidence sized to what exists | Method and a verified demonstration can carry trust without ornamental claim cards. | Apple’s numerical proof, client marks, testimonial format, or footnote treatment when Optimix has no corresponding evidence. |

Mobile translation is a **design requirement rather than a verified Apple finding**: preserve the reading order, labels, control points, and static workflow interpretation; do not collapse the process into anonymous cards or require horizontal dragging to learn the core sequence.

## Typeface research for EN / RO / RU

The preferred starting point is **one family across three languages**. That reduces inconsistent weight, x-height, and spacing when switching pages. The final decision must be made in rendered page comps containing real EN, RO, and RU text; font metadata alone cannot establish visual quality.

| Candidate | Authoritative coverage and license evidence | Variable / performance evidence | Design judgment and status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Onest** — first prototype candidate | The [maintainer README](https://github.com/simpals/onest/blob/main/README.md) describes screen readability, attention to diacritics, an August 2026 expansion of Cyrillic including Russian, and the [SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/simpals/onest/blob/main/OFL.txt). The [Google Fonts source metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/onest/METADATA.pb) lists Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended, and the `wght` axis. I downloaded Google Fonts’ current Onest variable TTF and checked its `cmap` using a small standard-library parser: all of `Ă ă Â â Î î Ș ș Ț ț Ё ё А а Я я` resolve to nonzero glyph IDs. The Russian paragraph in the maintainer’s [live specimen](https://simpals.github.io/onest/proof/diff3proof.html) also rendered in-browser. | The inspected TTF was 193,056 bytes and contained a `wght` variable axis; metadata states 100–900. Web delivery should later use a suitable self-hosted WOFF2 subset and keep the OFL notice. A raw TTF size is **not** a measured production transfer cost. | Geometric but human enough for operational clarity; distinguishable from routine SaaS Inter. The maintainer describes its own connection to Chişinău; this is context, not an implied relationship or endorsement. Prototype for headings/body/numerals; test whether its softer shapes still convey precision. **Provisional.** |
| **IBM Plex Sans** — engineering alternative | IBM’s [typeface specification](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/typeface/) and [repository](https://github.com/IBM/plex) document extended Latin and Cyrillic, with [OFL licensing](https://github.com/IBM/plex/blob/master/LICENSE.txt). The [Carbon font guide](https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon/blob/main/docs/guides/ibm-plex.md) documents Cyrillic splits and a variable package. | Current releases include `@ibm/plex-sans-variable`; check the exact chosen package and glyph subsets before bundling because static, variable, and script packages differ. I did **not** inspect the current binary for Romanian glyphs or measure its web weight. | Strong operational/technical character and distinctive numerals. Risk: its expression is closely associated with IBM; avoid copying IBM’s type scale or diagram style. **Alternative needing RO glyph and visual QA.** |
| **Inter** — legibility baseline | The [designer’s site](https://rsms.me/inter/) explicitly lists English, Romanian, and Russian, names the [SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt), and documents tabular numerals and screen-oriented drawing. | Official materials describe variable `wght` 100–900 and optical size `opsz` 14–32; downloadable variable and static files are both available from the [designer](https://rsms.me/inter/download/). | Excellent control sample for legibility and numbers; its ubiquity makes it less suitable as the main source of Optimix’s identity. Do not choose it merely to resemble Apple. **Baseline/fallback.** |

The glyph test proves presence of selected codepoints in one Onest binary. It does **not** prove the full Romanian or Russian typesetting quality, all punctuation/currency characters, web rendering on Windows/macOS/Android, or a finished font file strategy. In particular, Romanian must use comma-below `Ș ș Ț ț` (U+0218–U+021B), not cedilla substitutes.

### Recommendation and release test

Prototype **Onest as a single family** for headings, body, labels, and workflow notation; use tabular figures where columns genuinely compare measurements. Compare the same real content in IBM Plex Sans and Inter at desktop and mobile sizes. Keep practical body text comfortable and secondary text legible; the design should rely on spacing and hierarchy more than extreme weight differences. Do not lock exact font sizes, pairings, or a CDN now.

Before implementation, test the actual chosen release in all three languages with long headings, form labels, numerals, currency, arrows, and process stages. Include sample phrases such as `Optimize the handoff`, `Cerere → aprobare → comandă; Ș și Ț`, and `Запрос → согласование → заказ`; check Romanian comma-below, Russian `Ё/ё`, line wrapping, focus and zoom behavior, and browser font fallback. Measure the final WOFF2 transfers and layout shift once delivery is defined. Include the chosen font’s OFL notice with distributed assets.
