Target: a SINGLE complete long scrollable desktop page, 1440 px wide by approximately 6000 px tall. Flat orthographic webpage screenshot with no device frame, no browser chrome, no moodboard, no multiple alternatives, no perspective. No ornamental 3D, no stock photos, no floating glass cards, no purple/coral, no invented badges or figures. Most text sits on the page surface.
